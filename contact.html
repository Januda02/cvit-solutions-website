◆ FAQ

Common
questions.

Do you take on smaller projects? +

Yes — most of our work is for small businesses and independent educators. The form's budget field is a rough guide; if the fit is right on both sides, we'll figure it out.

Do you work with clients outside Sri Lanka? +

Yes. Most of our recent work has been with clients based outside Sri Lanka. We're comfortable working async and use video calls when needed.

How long does a typical project take? +

It varies with scope. A focused marketing site is measured in weeks; a platform build is measured in months. We agree the shape of the timeline together before starting.

Do you handle hosting and ongoing maintenance? +

We can — either as part of the initial build or as an ongoing retainer. If you'd rather host and maintain the site with your own team, we hand things over cleanly.
